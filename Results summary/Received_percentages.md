# Percentage of Received Payloads by CRDT Type


The experiments presented in this document were conducted over deliberately shortened durations in order to explore a wide range of configurations and analyse the scalability of the system under varying conditions. This file records the percentage of payloads successfully received across all peers in the system, serving as a transparency measure for our evaluation methodology. While these constrained durations may result in incomplete delivery in the most demanding configurations, this does not undermine our analysis — on the contrary, it surfaces the boundaries of each CRDT variant under stress, reinforcing and confirming the scalability claims made in the paper.

The graphs we presented in the paper are shown in the folder "Raw_Results", The complete set of data is too heavy to be shared here.

Values are shown as: **% at 100 updates - % at 500 updates**
